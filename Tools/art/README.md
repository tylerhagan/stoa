# Art scripts

Python (Pillow + NumPy) scripts that cut Stoa's console textures. The game never loads them.

- `chat_tabs.py <Media/Textures>` draws `ChatTab{Open,Closed}[Lines].tga` by bending the console edge's profile (`ConsoleEdge*.tga`) around a rounded tab.
- `plate_corners.py <Media/Textures>` draws `PlateFill.tga` and `PlateEdge.tga`, the small rounded pieces (3px corners, nine-sliced at 5px) that nameplate tracks and target edges use.
- `console_cut.py <source png> <Media/Textures> [ring] [width]` cuts `ConsoleEdge[Lines].tga` and `ConsoleMedallion[Lines].tga` (1024 wide) from the painting `source/console-basis.png` (painted over the line guide `source/console-guide-lines.png`; the first painting is `console-basis-first.png`, cut with ring 122 and width 512): scaled so the first ring out is 106 texels, the ring pulled round, both brass rings kept all the way under the seal, the long drop shadow trimmed to a soft edge. It prints the rows `Console.lua` uses. Rerun `chat_tabs.py` after it, since the tabs bend the edge.
- `round_medallion.py <src dir> <out dir>` (the older cut) takes the 512x256 medallion cut from the source art and stretches its ring into a true circle on a 512x512 canvas.

- Since 2026-10-29 the console comes from Tyler's flourish renders: the edge strip from `source/console-basis-flourish.png` (`console_cut.py source/console-basis-flourish.png ../../Media/Textures 124 1024 frame 1.35`; frame mode keeps the brackets, runs the painting's bottom line a short way, and lets the edge strip fade out just under the brass so the console's leather tile shows right up to it; only its `ConsoleEdge*` files are kept), then the medallion from his clean version without the leather band or bottom line, `source/console-medallion-clean.png` (`... console-medallion-clean.png ../../Media/Textures 124 1024 clean`, which leaves the edge strip alone and keeps the brackets' own ends). `console-basis-flourish-alt.png` is the other render. `medallion_flourish.py` drew the first procedural mock-up from Tyler's drawing (`source/medallion-flourish.png`; his full sketch is `source/medallion-frame.png`).
- `Wordmark.tga` (1024x256, white with alpha, tinted by the accent on the welcome page) and `Icon.tga` (64x64, the addon list icon) come from the logo files in `docs/logo/`.

Gold `*Lines` textures are white with alpha, and the theme accent tints them in game.
- `window_corners.py <Media/Textures>` draws the window and tooltip pieces: `WindowFill`/`WindowEdge` (10px corners), `WindowTop` and `WindowBottom` (header band and footer tray, rounded on one side), `CardTop` (a tooltip's header band) and `TabFill`/`TabEdge` (window tabs, square on top so they tuck under the window). All 32x32, nine-sliced at 12px.
- `minimap_frames.py <source dir> <Media/Textures>` cuts `MinimapFrameSquare[Lines].tga` and `MinimapFrameRing[Lines].tga` (512x512) from `source/minimap-square.png` and `source/minimap-ring.png`, centred on each frame's opening, and prints how much wider than the map each frame is (`PAINTED` in `Minimap.lua`).
- `coin_frame.py <source dir> <Media/Textures>` cuts `CoinFrame[Lines].tga` (128x128) from `source/coin-ring.png`, centred on the opening, and prints how much wider than the opening the frame is (`COIN_FRAME_SCALE` in `Core/Widgets.lua`). Used on portrait coins and the Halo's signature slot when the ornament is Painted.
- `hatch.py <Media/Textures>` draws `Hatch.tga` (16x16, thin tiling dark diagonal stripes, clear between): the DoT stretch on the target bar and nameplates (`Modules/Halo/Lethal.lua`).
- `shield.py <Media/Textures>` draws `Shield.tga` (32x32, a plain heater shield, white with alpha): the shield tag on party cards, filled by how much of the shield is left (`Core/Widgets.lua`).
- `cut_marks.py <source png> <Media/Textures>` cuts the painted silver marks (128x128) from Tyler's sheet `source/marks-silver.png`: `MarkShield` (your shield), `MarkFlame` (Inner Fire), `MarkHorn` (Battle Shout), `MarkDagger` (Slice and Dice), `MarkSeal` (paladin seals), `MarkOrb` (Lightning and Water Shield). Shown as painted, the drained part a darkened copy (`Core/Widgets.lua` UI.markArt, `Modules/Halo/Mark.lua`).
- `glyphs.py`: the small interface glyphs (GlyphCross, GlyphPlus, GlyphMinus, GlyphChevron, GlyphShift, GlyphDot), white with alpha and tinted in game.
- `line_flare.py <Media/Textures>` cuts `LineFlareLeft.tga` and `LineFlareRight.tga` (256x32 each): the masks that shape the Halo's ground line bars under Slate, round at the outer end and widening evenly toward the signature, ending just inside the dark seat it sits on.
- `plaque_dock.py <Media/Textures>` draws the restyled target plaque's pieces: `TargetBar.tga` and `TargetBarEdge.tga` (512x64, a pill the exact shape of the 228x20 health bar, used as its mask and outline).
- `plaque_join.py <Media/Textures>` draws `PlaqueJoin.tga` and `PlaqueJoinLines.tga` (320x288, shown at 80x72): the docked target plaque's right end, its gold top edge curving up into the portrait ring that rests on the console rim.
- `matte_grain.py [out]` draws `MatteGrain.tga` (128x128, a tiling pebble grain, each texel white or black with its strength in the alpha): the Matte material's surface on the console and windows (`Core/Widgets.lua` UI.GRAIN).
- `halo_arcs.py [Media/Textures]` draws the Halo's other arc styles, `HaloArc{Blade,Visor,Gauge,Tube}{Left,Right}.tga` (128x256, white with alpha): the Arc style setting (`Modules/Halo/Halo.lua` ARC_STYLES).
- `matte_tabs.py [Media/Textures]` draws the matte console's chat tabs, `ChatTabMatte{Open,Closed}[Lines].tga`, in the leather tabs' layout (`Modules/Grimoire/Housing.lua` tabFile).
- `matte_bezel.py [out]` draws `MatteBezel.tga` (256x256), the moulded ring round the Matte console's portrait: lip, tick channel and raised rim (`Console.lua` BuildSeal, BEZEL_SCALE).
- `matte_dock.py [Media/Textures]` draws the Matte ends of the docked target plaque and the target of target behind it (`PlaqueJoinMatte`, `PlaqueSocketMatte`, `PlaqueStepMatte` and their `Lines`), on the same canvases as `plaque_join.py` so `Core/Widgets.lua` places them alike.
- `matte_parts.py [Media/Textures]` draws the Matte material's moulded pieces, `MatteBlock.tga` (the bevel over cards, plaques and windows, `Core/Widgets.lua` UI.mould) and `MatteWell.tga` (the console's rounded recesses, `Console.lua` BuildWells); 32x32, nine-sliced at 12.
- `matte_key.py [Media/Textures]` draws the Matte plates: the small key `MatteKey.tga` (a flat plate with a 2 pixel corner, light top line, shade foot and dark underline; the Game Menu keys, `Modules/Skins/Windows.lua` raiseMenuKey) with `MatteKeyEdge.tga` (its outline for the hover), 16x16, nine-sliced at 5; the card `MatteCard.tga`, `MatteCardFill.tga` and `MatteCardState.tga` (the same plate with a 4 pixel corner for party cards and the target, tank watch, pet and target-of-target blocks), 24x24, nine-sliced at 8; and `MatteBarCorners.tga` (laid over a square health bar in the card's colour to round its corners by 2 pixels), 8x8, nine-sliced at 3.
- The Matte look's sharp set: `window_corners.py` also cuts every piece as `Sharp<Name>.tga` with a 4 pixel corner, and `matte_parts.py` draws `SharpBlock.tga` (fine light and shade lines in place of `MatteBlock.tga`'s soft bevel). `Core/Widgets.lua` swaps them in by name under Matte (the `SHARP` table by `sliced`).
- `slate_shadow.py <Media/Textures>` draws `SlateShadow.tga` (64x64, black with alpha): the soft drop shadow under each Slate console card, nine-sliced at 28 and laid 22 out from the card (`Console.lua` slatePlate).
