Classes

Every class gets a signature: a few coins on the Halo, an outline on your next press and a word when something needs you. Layouts follow your role; the signature follows your spec.

WarriorPaladinHunterRoguePriestShamanMageWarlockDruid

Warrior

Arms and Fury
Rend (R), Sunder Armor (S) and Battle Shout (B) coins with SUNDER n/5 and your stance named under them.
Protection
Sunder, Thunder Clap (T) and Demoralizing Shout (D), TAUNT over the ring when your group has the enemy's attention.
Next press
Shout, Rend, Mortal Strike or Bloodthirst, sunders, else the game's suggestion or Heroic Strike.

Paladin

Retribution
Coins on the Halo's right for your seal (S) and, once you've judged Crusader, Wisdom, Light or Justice, that Judgement mark on the target (J, forty seconds, kept fresh by Holy Strike with Sacred Arbiter).
Protection
Seal of Fury (S, amber while another seal runs), Holy Shield (H) and Consecration on the ground (C). An outline on the combat bar's next press for both. As a tank in a group, TAUNT over the ring when your target is fighting someone else (Judgement with Seal of Fury taunts). With Twist of Light, the moment a main-hand swing lands the other seal of the pair (Command and Righteousness) is outlined and named under the coins, so the next swing carries both. The signature slot watches Holy Strike, or Templar's Bulwark for tanks.

Hunter

Hunter's Mark (M), Serpent Sting (S) and Rapid Fire (RF) coins; NO ASPECT in the danger colour when none of yours is up.

Next press
Mark and Sting when down, else the game's suggestion or Arcane Shot (Raptor Strike for the Melee role). Your pet is the Pet row above.

Rogue

Slice and Dice (S), Rupture (R) and your big cooldown (Adrenaline Rush, Blade Flurry or Evasion) coins, COMBO n under them, pale gold at five.

Next press
Slice and Dice when it's down, Rupture or Eviscerate at five, else the game's suggestion or Sinister Strike.

Priest

Shadow
Coins on the Halo's right for Shadow Word: Pain (P), Devouring Plague (D) and Vampiric Embrace (V) on your target, draining as they run and in the warning colour when off. Timing is kept per target, so switching between mobs keeps each one's Pain. An outline on the combat bar's next press, which follows a /castsequence macro on your bars when there is one (the macro and the spell its next step casts both light); Mind Flay's three ticks on the cast line; SHADOWFORM over the ring when you're out of form; a PAIN n/m count of nameplates carrying your Pain; Shadow Weaving stacks on the target plaque. /stoa probe in a fight reports what the game hides from addons.
Discipline
Coins on the Halo's right for Penance (P), Inner Focus (I) and Power Infusion (PI), pale gold when ready and draining while they come back, bone while Inner Focus waits on your next spell; SHIELDS and RENEW counts of the group members carrying yours; an outline on Greater Heal while Inner Focus is up. The signature slot watches Penance.

Shaman

Flame Shock (F), your shield (S, with SHIELD n charges) and Earth Shock (E) coins; Restoration gets the shield, Nature's Swiftness (NS) and Mana Tide (MT). TOTEMS n/4 under them; the game's totem bar moves under the Halo instead of vanishing with the player frame.

Next press
Shield, Flame Shock, Stormstrike for Enhancement, else the game's suggestion, Earth Shock or Lightning Bolt.

Mage

Your tree is read from the talents you know.

Arcane
Arcane Power (AP), Presence of Mind (PM) and Evocation (E) coins, your Arcane Blast stacks, MISSILES while Missile Barrage is up.
Fire
Fire Blast (FB), Combustion (C) and, with Improved Scorch, Fire Vulnerability (S) coins; HOT STREAK n/3 with Pyroblast outlined at three.
Frost
Frost Nova (N), Ice Barrier (B) and Ice Block (IB) coins; FINGERS while Fingers of Frost is up, Ice Lance outlined then and right after each Frostbolt. NO ARMOR and NO INTELLECT reminders.

Warlock

Corruption (C), Curse of Agony (A) and Immolate (I) coins timed from your casts when the game hides them; SHARDS n from your bags and TAP when mana is low and health can spare it.

Next press
Life Tap, whichever DoT is down, else the game's suggestion or Shadow Bolt.

Druid

Follows your form first, then your role.

Balance
Moonfire (M) and Insect Swarm (I) coins, LUNAR or SOLAR while an Eclipse is up, and the next press as two Starfires to one Wrath unless the Eclipse says otherwise.
Cat
Rip (R), Rake (K) and Faerie Fire (F) coins and your combo points; Shred outlined on Clearcasting, Shifting Power when energy runs low.
Bear
Lacerate (L, with its stacks), Demoralizing Roar (D) and Faerie Fire coins, TAUNT with Growl outlined.
Restoration
Swiftmend (S), Wild Growth (W) and Nature's Swiftness (N) coins, REJUV and REGROWTH counts on the group, and REJUV, REGROWTH and GROWTH on party cards. Out of the form your role wants in a fight, its name shows over the ring. Timers use classic lengths until Forever's are known; the game's own times win wherever it shows them.

For every class

Halo
In combat: health and mana arcs, signature spell with cooldown, target and cast bar around your character.
Procs
The game's own proc art either side of the Halo and its proc glow on the combat bar button, from the game's proc events and from STOA's own reading (usable, free or faster casts) while buffs are hidden. Procs nothing readable changes (Redoubt, Vengeance, Martyrdom...) are small game-drawn coins above the Halo. Art found by the game's file ID; STOA's own thin arc if a picture is missing; /stoa procs test, /stoa procs list.
Combat bar
In combat, a compact row of your main bar's spells under the Halo: rounded icons, keys underneath, cooldown sweeps; dimmed when unusable, blue when short of mana, faded with its key in the warning colour when out of range. Up to two more rows follow from the bottom-left and bottom-right bars, each a step smaller (Settings: Combat rows; healers default to one). Spells on a long cooldown sit dim until they're ready.
Coin art
Each Halo coin carries its spell's icon faintly behind the letter, in colour while it runs or is ready and grey while it's off or coming back (a setting)
